MasterMagneticAnisotropyEnergy

Calculates an integral over the uniaxial magnetic anisotropy energy density -K1 (m.n)^2 - K2 (m.n)^4. The material property K2 is optional (taken as zero if no material provides it).

Overview

Computes the free energy of the simulation box (volume ) corresponding to magnetocrystalline anisotropy,

where is the normalized magnetization vector and , the anisotropy constants (: easy axis along , the convention of the kernel MasterAnisotropyCartLLG). The material property K2 is optional and is taken as zero when no material provides it. The vector is the normalized director of the anisotropy. Typically, this is denoted as in the literature but in order to avoid confusion with the commonly referred surface normal in the MOOSE code, we choose . The units of the energy are given by units of but an optional input parameter flag energyscale is provided allowing to quickly convert the postprocessed units.
