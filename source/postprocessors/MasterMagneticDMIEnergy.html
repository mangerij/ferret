MasterMagneticDMIEnergy

Calculates an integral over the bulk or interfacial Dzyaloshinskii-Moriya energy density of a ferromagnet.

Overview

Computes the free energy of the simulation box (volume ) corresponding to the Dzyaloshinskii-Moriya interaction of a ferromagnet,

selected by dmi_type, with the same sign convention as the kernel MasterDMICartLLG. The units of the energy are given by units of times the mesh volume, and the optional input parameter energyscale converts them (e.g. for a mesh in nm with D supplied as , giving J).

Example Input File Syntax

The DMI class is taken from dmi_type (set in [GlobalParams] in this example):

[Postprocessors<<<{"href": "../../syntax/Postprocessors/index.html"}>>>]
  [E_dmi]
    type = MasterMagneticDMIEnergy<<<{"description": "Calculates an integral over the bulk or interfacial Dzyaloshinskii-Moriya energy density of a ferromagnet.", "href": "MasterMagneticDMIEnergy.html"}>>>
    energy_scale<<<{"description": "the energy scale, useful for transition between eV and aJ"}>>> = 1e-27
  []
[]
(test/tests/magnets/sp6_1d.i)

It enters the total energy with the other terms:

[Postprocessors<<<{"href": "../../syntax/Postprocessors/index.html"}>>>]
  [E_total]
    type = LinearCombinationPostprocessor<<<{"description": "Computes a linear combination between an arbitrary number of post-processors", "href": "LinearCombinationPostprocessor.html"}>>>
    pp_names<<<{"description": "List of post-processors"}>>> = 'E_exchange E_dmi E_anisotropy'
    pp_coefs<<<{"description": "List of linear combination coefficients for each post-processor"}>>> = '1 1 1'
  []
[]
(test/tests/magnets/sp6_1d.i)
