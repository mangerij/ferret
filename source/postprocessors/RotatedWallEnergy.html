RotatedWallEnergy

Calculates an integral over the gradient (Ginzburg) energy of a cubic parent phase, with the crystal axes turned by the material property twist about z (the energy of RotatedWallEnergyDerivative).

Overview

Integrates the gradient energy of RotatedWallEnergyDerivative (bare coefficients G11, G12, G44) for a cubic crystal whose axes are turned about by the material property twist.

Example Input File Syntax

[./FgP]
  type = RotatedWallEnergy
  execute_on = 'timestep_end'
  block = '0'
[../]
(test/tests/flexo/flexo_180DW_theta15.i)
