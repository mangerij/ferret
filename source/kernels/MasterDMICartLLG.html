MasterDMICartLLG

Calculates a residual contribution due to the bulk or interfacial (Cnv, normal +z) Dzyaloshinskii-Moriya interaction of a ferromagnet. The weak form is not integrated by parts, so the DMI surface term is retained and the exchange + DMI natural boundary condition holds without a BC object.
