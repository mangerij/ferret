MasterAnisotropyCartLLG

Calculates a residual contribution for the uniaxial magnetic anisotropy energy -K1 (m.n)^2 - K2 (m.n)^4 (K1 > 0: easy axis along n). The material property K2 is optional (taken as zero if no material provides it). Optional hard-axis term Kh (m.nh)^2 (Kh > 0: hard axis along nh; material properties Kh, nhx, nhy, nhz) in both routes; it has no |m|^2 part, so with nh perpendicular to n it leaves |m| = m_e in the domains of the LLB route.
